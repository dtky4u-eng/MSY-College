[Heading1] RKNexora ERP — Application Requirements Specification
Sep 26, 2026 · @Deepak Kumar
[Heading2] 1. Introduction
RKNexora ERP is a web-based internship management system that runs the full NEP 2020 / CBCS undergraduate internship lifecycle — from college-verified student registration and online fee payment to learning, attendance, assessment and QR-verified certificates.
Purpose. This specification records what the application does today, reverse-engineered from the public site (rknexora.org), the registration and login flows, and the client-side route and API surface. It is meant as a baseline for QA, change requests and new-feature scoping.
Scope. Public marketing site, student self-registration (6 steps), authentication, and four role portals: Student, College, Mentor and Admin. Authenticated screens were analysed from their UI definitions, not from live data, since no test credentials were used.
Business context.
[ListParagraph] Operator: RK NEXORA PRIVATE LIMITED (MSME registered, MCA incorporated; claims AICTE alignment and ISO 9001:2015).
[ListParagraph] Target users: 4-year CBCS UG students who must complete an internship of about 120 hours for 4 academic credits.
[ListParagraph] Revenue model: students pay a domain-wise internship fee online; each partner college earns a configurable revenue share, settled offline by RKNexora.
[ListParagraph] Stated reach: 10,000+ students, 200+ partner colleges, 50+ internship domains.
Tech stack observed. Next.js (App Router) front end with Tailwind CSS; REST back end under role-scoped endpoints; Razorpay and Cashfree payment gateways; bilingual UI (English + Hindi) on student-facing screens.
[Heading2] 2. User roles and access
The system has four authenticated roles plus anonymous visitors; login routes each user to /student, /college, /mentor or /admin based on the role returned by the server.
Role | Created by | Core responsibilities | Data scope
Visitor | — | Browse domains, process and compliance info; start registration | Public content only
Student | Self-registers after college record match | Pay fee, learn, attend, submit work, download documents | Own record only
College | Admin ("Add College" creates college + college-admin login) | Upload student database, track registrations, payments, progress; reset student passwords; download certificates | Students of own college
Mentor | Admin ("Add Mentor", tied to one domain) | Manage chapter resources and quizzes, review submissions, assess students, grant quiz reattempts | Assigned students, own domain
Admin (RKNexora) | System | Master data, colleges, mentors, students, internships, payments, settlements, live classes, routines, messaging, bulk automation, reports | All data
Access rules
[ListParagraph] AR-1: Login accepts username, email or RK Nexora registration number, plus password.
[ListParagraph] AR-2: Sessions use access + refresh tokens (/auth/refresh); protected routes show "Restoring session…" then redirect to /login if unauthenticated.
[ListParagraph] AR-3: An unknown role in the login response must be rejected with an error.
[ListParagraph] AR-4: Forgot-password sends a reset link to the registered email; reset is completed via /auth/reset-password.
[ListParagraph] AR-5: College users may change passwords only for students of their own college.
[ListParagraph] AR-6: Mentors see only students assigned to them and only chapters in their domain.

[Heading2] 4. Functional requirements
Requirements are numbered by module; each reflects behaviour or validation present in the current build.
[Heading3] 4.1 Public website
[ListParagraph] FR-PUB-1: Show hero with "Apply for Internship" (to /register) and "Explore Domains" (anchor).
[ListParagraph] FR-PUB-2: List top domains: Web Development, AI & ML, Data Analytics, Digital Marketing, Business Management, Human Resources, Project Management, Agri-Business.
[ListParagraph] FR-PUB-3: Explain the 8-step journey: Registration, Document Verification, Domain Selection, Mentor Allocation, Training & Live Projects, Attendance & Logbook, Report & Evaluation, Certificate.
[ListParagraph] FR-PUB-4: Explain NEP 2020 value: mandatory, 4 credits, about 120 hours, report and certificate.
[ListParagraph] FR-PUB-5: Show contact (+91 9693275424, helpdesk@rknexora.org) and legal policy links.
[Heading3] 4.2 Student registration (6-step wizard, bilingual)
[ListParagraph] Verify — Student enters the University/College Registration Number from the admit card. The system matches it against the college-uploaded record and resumes from the last saved step. Roll number, mobile or RK Nexora number must not be accepted.
[ListParagraph] Details — Name is pre-filled from the college record. Student confirms Father Name, Gender (Male/Female/Other), DOB, Programme, Major Subject, Session (e.g. 2023-27, 2024-28), Semester (4, 5). Student sets Mobile (10 digits), Email, Username (min 4 chars), Password (min 8) and Confirm Password.
[ListParagraph] Domain — Student picks one domain; any stream may choose any domain. Screen shows fee, duration (hours) and "Certificate Included". Fee is the college-specific fee if set, else the default.
[ListParagraph] Documents — Passport photo and latest semester admit card are mandatory.
[ListParagraph] Review — Student confirms all data. Confirmation locks details and documents permanently.
[ListParagraph] Payment — System creates a gateway order (Razorpay or Cashfree, chosen by server). Account activates only after server-side verification. A success screen offers Download Receipt and Go to Login.
[ListParagraph] FR-REG-1: Locked registrations cannot be edited; already-paid users are sent to login.
[ListParagraph] FR-REG-2: Handle "payment received, awaiting confirmation" and "paid but verification failed — contact support with payment ID" states.
[ListParagraph] FR-REG-3: RK Nexora Registration Number becomes active after payment.
[Heading3] 4.3 Student portal
[ListParagraph] FR-STU-1 Dashboard: internship status, start date, course progress, hours remaining, overall progress; summaries for learning, quizzes, logbook, attendance, live project, report, certificate eligibility; admin announcements; recent activity.
[ListParagraph] FR-STU-2 Access states: Waiting to Start (no start date), Not Started (future date), Active, Completed (attendance locked), Blocked.
[ListParagraph] FR-STU-3 Attendance: daily Check In / Check Out; statuses Present, Half Day, Absent, Approved leave, Not Marked; monthly calendar, percentage, history with date/status search.
[ListParagraph] FR-STU-4 Learning: modules and chapters unlock sequentially. Chapters have recorded lecture (minimum watch time), YouTube, PDF, notes or external links, and a reading-time requirement. A chapter completes by "Mark Complete" or by passing its quiz.
[ListParagraph] FR-STU-5 Quizzes: passing score, limited attempts, optional time limit; Start / Continue / Retry / View Result.
[ListParagraph] FR-STU-6 Engagement: send heartbeats while learning so the server can count active learning hours.
[ListParagraph] FR-STU-7 Assignments: view due date and max marks, download brief, upload PDF/DOC/DOCX/PPT/PPTX/ZIP up to 10 MB. See mentor feedback and resubmit unless approved.
[ListParagraph] FR-STU-8 Logbook: daily entry with hours worked and skills learned.
[ListParagraph] FR-STU-9 Live project: title, project report and photos. Internship report: upload final signed report.
[ListParagraph] FR-STU-10 Routine: view and download the active published routine (PDF/image).
[ListParagraph] FR-STU-11 Download Center: Offer Letter, Acceptance Letter, Attendance Sheet, Digital Logbook, Internship Report, Assessment Marksheet, Internship Certificate, and payment receipts.
[ListParagraph] FR-STU-12 Profile: read-only personal, academic and internship data. Student can edit only mobile (10–15 digits), email and profile photo URL.
[ListParagraph] FR-STU-13 Notifications: unread count, mark read, mark all read. Upcoming live classes with join/leave tracking and a popup before start.
[Heading3] 4.4 College portal
[ListParagraph] FR-COL-1 Dashboard: total students, active internships, average progress, college revenue. Also pending students, mentor assigned, 12-month registration trend, domain distribution, payment summary, revenue split (total vs RKNexora share), monthly and domain-wise revenue, recent students.
[ListParagraph] FR-COL-2 Upload: import students from one .xlsx/.xls file up to 10 MB using the template. Show total rows, imported count and row-level warnings.
[ListParagraph] FR-COL-3 Students & Certificates: search by name, registration, email or mobile. View and download certificates; change a student's password.
[ListParagraph] FR-COL-4 Registrations: track registration status of uploaded students.
[ListParagraph] FR-COL-5 Payments: student collection, earned share, amount received, pending balance, settlement progress, and payment history (date, mode such as bank transfer, transaction no.).
[ListParagraph] FR-COL-6 Profile: edit name, university, principal, coordinator, mobile (10 digits), email, state, district, pincode (6 digits), address. College code is not editable.
[Heading3] 4.5 Mentor portal
[ListParagraph] FR-MEN-1 Dashboard: assigned, active and completed students; pending reviews and assessments; average progress; students with quiz attempts exhausted; recently assigned students.
[ListParagraph] FR-MEN-2 Resources: add, edit, delete and reorder chapter resources in own domain. Types: video, PDF, text/notes, external link, source code, other. Fields: title, sort order (min 1), primary flag, file.
[ListParagraph] FR-MEN-3 Quizzes: create per chapter with passing score 0–100%, attempts (min 1), time limit, randomize and show-result options. Each question has options, a correct answer, marks and an optional explanation.
[ListParagraph] FR-MEN-4 Quiz reattempts: grant extra attempts to assigned students.
[ListParagraph] FR-MEN-5 Reviews: review submissions, add comments, approve or request resubmission.
[ListParagraph] FR-MEN-6 Assessments: rate technical knowledge, problem solving and more (Very Good to Needs Improvement). Add supervisor remarks and recommend a certificate.
[Heading3] 4.6 Admin portal
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-1 Dashboard: KPIs for students, colleges, mentors, domains, active internships, completion rate, payment rate, unassigned, paid, pending and blocked students, pending colleges; charts and top colleges.
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-2 Messaging: send a titled message to all paid students or selected paid students.
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-3 Colleges: CRUD. Create sets code, university, principal, coordinator, contacts, address, logo (JPG/PNG/WEBP, max 2 MB), revenue share (college % + RKNexora % = 100) and a college-admin login.
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-4 Domain fees: per college, custom fee per domain (must be > 0) or fall back to the default fee. Supports bulk set and reset.
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-5 Mentors: CRUD with name, employee ID, mobile, email, domain, optional college, photo URL and login (username defaults to employee ID). Assign or remove same-domain students.
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-6 Students: filter by college, session, status and payment. Import Excel mapped to a college. Edit details, photo (max 2 MB), status and internship dates (end on or after start). Change password.
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-7 Internships: set start date for paid students singly or in bulk with a common start date.
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-8 Learning setup: sectors, then domains (with duration hours), modules, chapters, resources and quizzes. Quizzes can be imported from Excel.
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-9 Payments: search and filter all payments; view gateway IDs; generate or regenerate receipts. Manual "Mark Paid" or edit is allowed only for created payments and needs a reason of at least 10 characters.
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-10 College settlements: record a payment (amount up to remaining balance, UTR/cheque ref, remarks, proof) and keep settlement history.
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-11 Live classes: schedule by domain, module or chapter with title, meeting link, date/time, trainer, duration and popup lead time.
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-12 Routines: upload PDF/JPG/PNG/WEBP up to 10 MB with title, description, publish date/time and active or hidden status.
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-13 Bulk automation: filter students, preview (matched students, working days, estimated records), then run as a tracked job with cancel and retry. Operations: full lifecycle, attendance, learning completion, assessment, results, completion, offer letters, attendance sheets, logbooks, reports, QR certificates, ZIP.
[ListParagraph] FR-ADM-14 Reports: Registration, Attendance, Completion and Payment reports. Filter by college, domain, session, semester, internship and payment status, and date range. Preview with pagination and export to Excel or PDF.
[Heading2] 5. Data entities and key fields
Fourteen core entities carry the domain; field names below are those used by the client where visible.
Entity | Key fields | Relationships / rules
College | name, code (immutable), university, principal, coordinator, email, mobile, state, district, pincode, address, logo, college_share %, rknexora_share % | Has one college-admin user; has many students; shares must total 100
User | username, email, password, role (admin / college / mentor / student) | Login by username, email or registration number
Student | student_id, registration_number (university), portal_registration_number (RK Nexora), name, father_name, gender, dob, programme, major_subject, session, semester, mobile, email, photo, identity_document, marksheet | Belongs to college, domain, mentor; flags registration_locked, next_step
Sector | name | Has many domains
Domain | domain_id, domain_name, duration_hours, default fee | Belongs to sector; has modules; overridable per college
College domain fee | college, domain, college fee | Empty value means use default fee; effective fee = college fee or default
Module / Chapter | module number + name; chapter number + name | Sequential unlock; chapter has resources and quiz
Resource | title, type (video, PDF, notes, link, code, other), file or URL, sort order, primary, downloadable | Belongs to chapter
Quiz | title, description, passing score %, attempts allowed, time limit, randomize, show result | Questions with options, correct answer, marks, explanation; attempts; reattempt grants
Attendance | date, check-in, check-out, status (present, half day, absent, leave), remarks, learning hours | One row per student per day
Logbook / Assignment / Project / Report | logbook: date, hours, skills; assignment: due date, max marks, submission, feedback, marks, status; project: title, report, photos; report: file | Reviewed by mentor
Assessment | criteria ratings, supervisor remarks, certificate recommendation, result, grade | Feeds certificate eligibility
Payment | transaction_id, order_id, razorpay_order_id / payment_id / signature, cashfree_order_id / transaction_id, amount, method, payment_status (created, pending, success/paid, failed, refunded), failure_reason, paid_at, receipt number / path | One student, many attempts
College settlement | college, amount, mode, UTR / reference, remarks, proof, date | Cannot exceed remaining earned share
Live class, Routine, Notification, Bulk job | class: domain / module / chapter, link, trainer, time, duration, popup minutes; routine: title, file, publish time, active; notification: title, body, read; job: type, filters, progress, status, result ZIP | —
Internship status values seen: pending, active, completed, blocked; the student UI also shows "waiting to start" and "not started" states derived from the start date.
[Heading2] 6. Key business workflows
The core flow runs college onboarding → student data upload → self-registration and payment → internship start → delivery → evaluation → certificates → college settlement.
[Code] flowchart LR  A[Admin adds college + fees] --> B[College uploads student Excel]  B --> C[Student verifies reg. no.]  C --> D[Details, domain, documents]  D --> E[Review and lock]  E --> F[Online payment]  F --> G[Admin assigns mentor + start date]  G --> H[Learning, attendance, logbook]  H --> I[Assignments, project, report]  I --> J[Mentor review + assessment]  J --> K[Results + QR certificate]  K --> L[Admin settles college share]
WF-1 Payment and activation
[ListParagraph] Registration is locked, then the server creates an order and returns the gateway (Razorpay or Cashfree).
[ListParagraph] Checkout opens on the client; on return, the server verifies the signature or order status.
[ListParagraph] On success, the account activates, a receipt is generated and the portal registration number becomes active.
[ListParagraph] Failed or unverified payments can be retried; admins can reconcile and mark paid with a reason.
WF-2 Learning progression
[ListParagraph] A chapter unlocks only after the previous one is complete.
[ListParagraph] The student meets the lecture watch time and reading time, tracked through heartbeats.
[ListParagraph] The chapter completes via its quiz pass, or via "Mark Complete" when no quiz exists.
[ListParagraph] When attempts run out, the mentor or admin can grant a reattempt.
WF-3 Submission review
[ListParagraph] The student submits an assignment, project or report.
[ListParagraph] The item shows as Pending Review; the mentor approves it or requests resubmission with comments.
[ListParagraph] Approved items are locked.
WF-4 Completion and certification
[ListParagraph] The mentor submits the assessment and recommends a certificate.
[ListParagraph] Admin publishes results and marks the internship complete, manually or through bulk automation.
[ListParagraph] Documents are generated: offer letter, attendance sheet, logbook, report, marksheet, QR-verified certificate.
[ListParagraph] Documents appear in the student Download Center; colleges can download certificates.
WF-5 College settlement
[ListParagraph] Earned share = successful student payments × college share %.
[ListParagraph] Admin records settlements (bank transfer, UTR) up to the pending balance.
[ListParagraph] The college sees settlement progress and history.
[Heading2] 7. Non-functional requirements
The build already sets file limits, role isolation and server-side payment checks; the items below formalise those and add the expectations an ERP of this kind needs.
ID | Category | Requirement
NFR-1 | Security | Role-based access enforced on the server for every endpoint, not only by client routing
NFR-2 | Security | Passwords at least 8 characters, stored hashed; token refresh and logout invalidate sessions
NFR-3 | Payments | Activation only after server-side signature or webhook verification; idempotent retries; no card data stored
NFR-4 | Data integrity | Registration and approved submissions immutable after lock; manual payment edits need a reason and are logged
NFR-5 | Audit | Log admin actions on payments, student status, passwords, bulk jobs and settlements with user and timestamp
NFR-6 | File handling | Excel imports max 10 MB (.xlsx/.xls); assignments and routines max 10 MB; photos and logos max 2 MB (JPG/PNG/WEBP); type checks on server as well
NFR-7 | Performance | Paginated lists and reports; bulk jobs run asynchronously with progress, cancel and retry
NFR-8 | Usability | Bilingual (English/Hindi) student flows; responsive layout for mobile, since most students register by phone
NFR-9 | Localisation | Consistent language in messages: some college and admin screens mix Hinglish (e.g. "Profile load nahi hua")
NFR-10 | Verification | Certificates carry a QR code that resolves to a public verification page
NFR-11 | Privacy | Personal data and documents (ID, marksheet, photo) visible only to the student, their college, assigned mentor and admin; policy pages published
NFR-12 | Availability | Payment and registration paths monitored; clear recovery path if a payment succeeds but verification fails

Observed gaps

Open questions

[ListParagraph] ☐ Is a refund flow in scope? The status "refunded" exists but there is no UI for it.
[ListParagraph] ☐ What are the exact certificate eligibility rules (minimum hours, attendance %, quiz average, approvals)?
[ListParagraph] ☐ Can colleges self-register, or is onboarding admin-only?
[ListParagraph] ☐ Should mentors be able to mark attendance or approve leave ("Approved leave" status exists)?
[ListParagraph] ☐ What are the retention period and consent basis for student ID documents?
